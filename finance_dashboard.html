The benchmark freezes whatever the Sandbox shows right now and projects it from your “investing since” month, so you can see whether you’re ahead of or behind the plan you committed to. Re-set it any time your plan changes.

Monthly market change

Each bar is the gain or loss between two months where you recorded a value, with that period’s contributions removed — so it reflects market movement, not deposits. Use the arrows to move the window 6 months at a time.
